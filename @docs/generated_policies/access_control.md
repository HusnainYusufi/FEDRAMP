# Access Control Policy

## Purpose
Define access control requirements for the system.

## Scope
Applies to all workforce members, contractors, and system components.

## Policy
- Authentication shall use MFA where supported.
- Access shall follow least privilege.
- Privileged access shall be reviewed at least quarterly.

## Technologies
- IAM: AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM)
- OS: TBD

## Roles and Responsibilities
- Security: Define and review access requirements.
- IT/Ops: Implement IAM policies and enforce MFA.

## Review Cadence
This policy is reviewed at least annually.
